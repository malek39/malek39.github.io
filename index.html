01 / About

A data scientist who treats infrastructure as part of the science.

I started in Big Data Engineering at Huawei — building Hadoop ingestion pipelines for telecom Customer Experience Management platforms, debugging Core Network trace data, learning that analytical insight is only useful if the underlying system is reliable. I then led the analytics function for a major operator's SmartCare programme, scaled a cross-functional team, and built churn, segmentation, and migration models that contributed directly to retention and revenue.

At Rakuten Viber I worked on global product analytics for a billion-user messaging app — driving feature investment decisions across markets and contributing to +10% user base, +5% MAU, +7.5% revenue. At Heetch, I now operate as a strategic data scientist embedded in the Growth org: architecting the analytics layer for a new two-sided bidding marketplace, owning the Bayesian CLV system, and acting as the primary data partner to the VP of Growth.

My work spans marketplace analytics, Bayesian modeling and probabilistic inference, A/B experimentation, MLOps on GCP Vertex AI, and analytics engineering. Below is a selection of projects across three companies and seven years.
